# Panda Hobby Customer Support FAQ

Use this document for factual answers the AI can rely on when drafting support replies.

## Orders and shipping

### How quickly do orders ship out?

- Orders are usually prepared within 24 to 48 hours.
- Panda Hobby usually ships the same day or next business day when possible.
- Orders are shipped Monday to Friday.
- Orders do not ship on weekends or postal holidays.
- Delays can happen if the name or address needs to be corrected before shipment.

### How long does shipping take within Canada?

- Canadian shipments usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.
- Best estimates by province:
- Ontario: 1 to 3 business days
- Quebec: 2 to 5 business days
- New Brunswick: 5 to 7 business days
- Nova Scotia: 5 to 7 business days
- PEI: 6 to 8 business days
- Newfoundland: 7 to 10 business days
- Manitoba: 4 to 6 business days
- Saskatchewan: 7 to 10 business days
- Alberta: 5 to 7 business days
- British Columbia: 5 to 7 business days
- Some areas, including British Columbia in certain cases, may take 7 to 14 business days.
- Shipping times are estimates only and may change due to carrier delays, holidays, or other disruptions.

### What should we say if tracking has not updated yet?

- A tracking number can be issued before the carrier tracking page begins showing movement.
- Some shipments are first moved to a local shipping hub and then handed off to Canada Post for final delivery.
- Because of that handoff, tracking may not update until the last few days of the shipment.
- If a tracking number has been assigned, the parcel has already been shipped and is on the way.
- If no tracking number has been sent yet, the courier may still be processing the tracking information.

### What should we say if a customer says the order is taking too long?

- Ask the customer to check whether the order includes a pre-order item.
- Ask the customer to check whether the order has been fulfilled and whether a tracking number is visible.
- Ask the customer to check their email in case Panda Hobby requested information needed to complete the order.
- If the order has tracking, direct them to the tracking link first.
- If that does not resolve the issue, direct them to customerservice@pandahobby.ca for manual review.

### What are the Canadian shipping rates?

- Standard rates for most Canadian addresses:
- $15 flat rate
- $10 shipping on orders over $150 before tax
- Free shipping on orders over $300 before tax
- Areas requiring actual shipping rates include Newfoundland and Labrador, Northwest Territories, Nunavut, and Yukon.
- Some remote or rural addresses may require an additional shipping fee.
- Large items require actual shipping rates.
- Heavily discounted or clearance items require actual shipping rates.

### What does expedited carriers shipping mean?

- Expedited shipping costs more than the standard flat-rate shipping fee.
- Orders may be sent using carriers such as UPS, FedEx, Canpar, Purolator, ICS, DHL, or similar providers.
- If the shipping fee to the customer’s address is unusually high, Panda Hobby may contact the customer to request an additional fee or offer a different courier option.

### Can orders ship to PO boxes?

- Panda Hobby does not ship to PO boxes by default.
- PO box shipping may be approved on a case-by-case basis.
- Customers should email before placing the order if they require PO box delivery.
- Orders may be canceled automatically if a PO box is used without prior approval.

### What is an oversized item?

- Oversized items are items that are too large for regular shipping boxes or too heavy for flat-rate shipping.
- Orders containing oversized items require the customer to pay the actual shipping cost.
- If the shipping cost is too high, the customer may cancel that order and receive a refund for that order.

## USA shipping

### How does USA shipping work?

- Flat-rate USA shipping is no longer offered.
- Customers first pay $30 CAD at checkout.
- After the order is prepared, Panda Hobby sends a PayPal invoice for the remaining balance for shipping and customs if needed.
- USA orders require actual shipping rates plus customs prepaid before shipment.

### Why are customs charged for USA shipments?

- Current USA shipping policy requires inbound shipments to have customs or tariff charges prepaid before entry.
- If customs are not prepaid, there is a high chance the parcel may be rejected at customs.

### Can paints, liquids, or spray cans be shipped to the USA?

- As of October 2025, paints, spray cans, and liquids cannot be shipped to the USA.
- If an order contains only restricted items, the order will be canceled and refunded.
- If an order contains restricted and unrestricted items, Panda Hobby will contact the customer to exchange the restricted item before shipping.

### Can a customer request a specific courier for USA shipping?

- Yes, customers can add their preferred courier in the checkout notes.
- Panda Hobby will quote based on the requested courier when possible.
- Final courier choice may still depend on the customer’s address and available rates.

## International shipping outside Canada and the USA

### How does international shipping work?

- Customers outside Canada and the USA pay $20 CAD at checkout.
- After the order is prepared, Panda Hobby sends a PayPal invoice for the remaining shipping balance.
- If the customer accepts the shipping rate, they pay the invoice and the order ships.
- If the customer does not want to proceed, the order can be canceled and refunded.
- Transit time may range from one week to a couple of months depending on destination and shipping speed.

### Can paints, liquids, or spray cans be shipped internationally?

- Panda Hobby may contact the customer if the order contains paints, spray cans, or liquids.
- These items may be refused by the destination country’s customs authority.
- If the parcel is returned and Panda Hobby receives it back, the items may be refunded minus shipping paid.
- If the parcel is seized or destroyed by customs, Panda Hobby cannot refund the order.
- Customers must accept this risk before shipping restricted items internationally.

## Pre-orders

### What does pre-order mean?

- A pre-order is an item that has not yet been released but is scheduled for production.
- Customers pay at the time they place the pre-order.
- Pre-order customers are fulfilled first before any leftover stock is released for general sale.
- If Panda Hobby receives fewer units than expected, orders are fulfilled in order-time sequence.

### When will a pre-order ship or be ready for pickup?

- If an order contains a pre-order, the full order can only ship or be picked up when all items in that order are available.
- Partial pickup and partial shipment are not available on mixed pre-order orders.
- If the customer wants in-stock items sooner, they must place those items in a separate order.

### When will pre-order items arrive?

- Each pre-order product page lists the estimated release period provided by the manufacturer or vendor.
- Estimated release dates are subject to change or delay without notice.
- Panda Hobby is not notified in advance of exact arrival dates.
- Once the item arrives at Panda Hobby’s warehouse, pickup customers receive a ready-for-pickup email and shipping customers are processed for shipment.

### What do the pre-order title statuses mean?

- `[Pre-Order]` means Panda Hobby is still accepting pre-orders for the item.
- `[Pre-Order End]` means the pre-order window has closed and no new pre-orders are being accepted.
- `[Coming Soon]` means the shipment has landed in Canada and is expected at the warehouse within 1 to 3 weeks.
- `[Arrived]` means the pre-order item has arrived at the warehouse and is being prepared for customers.
- `[Partial Arrival]` means only part of the requested stock arrived, and affected customers may receive separate updates.

### Is there a pre-order status page?

- Yes. Panda Hobby has a Pre-Order Status Page for arrival and status updates.

### How do preorder deposits work?

- A preorder deposit is a partial payment used to reserve an item before arrival.
- Once the item arrives, Panda Hobby sends an invoice for the remaining balance.
- After the balance is paid, the order can be shipped or made ready for pickup.
- If Panda Hobby cannot receive the preorder item from the supplier, the deposit is issued as store credit.
- Preorders cannot be canceled once the deposit has been paid.
- If the remaining balance is not paid within the allowed period after notification, Panda Hobby may cancel the preorder and apply the deposit as store credit.

## Pickup and vault

### How does in-store pickup work?

- Customers choose pickup during checkout and select their pickup location.
- Panda Hobby first sends an order confirmation email.
- When the order is prepared, Panda Hobby sends a READY FOR PICKUP email.
- Customers should wait for the READY FOR PICKUP email before coming to the store.

### How long does pickup preparation take?

- During operating hours, pickup preparation may take up to 2 hours.
- If the store is closed, the order is prepared once the store reopens.
- Customers do not need to pick up on the same day.

### What if the customer accidentally chose pickup instead of shipping?

- Panda Hobby can send a shipping invoice if the customer replies to the READY FOR PICKUP email.
- Customers can also place a separate order, pay the correct shipping, and leave a note asking for the orders to be combined.

### What is Panda Vault?

- Panda Vault is a private warehouse option that lets customers hold and combine orders to save on shipping.
- Vault orders can be combined later and shipped together based on the combined order value before tax.
- Orders can stay in the Vault for 60 days.

### How does a customer ship their Vault orders?

- The customer requests shipment using the Vault request form.
- If the combined order qualifies for free shipping, Panda Hobby packs and ships it within 24 to 48 hours.
- If the combined order does not qualify for free shipping, Panda Hobby either sends a shipping invoice or the customer can place another order and leave a note saying which orders to combine or simply “Ship My Vault.”

### Can a Vault order be canceled?

- No. Orders placed into the Vault cannot be canceled or refunded.

### What if an order has not shipped because the customer chose Vault by mistake?

- The customer can request a shipping invoice.
- Local customers can pick the order up in-store.
- The customer can place a second order, select the correct shipping option, and leave a note asking Panda Hobby to combine the orders.

## Returns, refunds, and exchanges

### What is the return window?

- Eligible returns or exchanges are accepted up to 14 days after purchase.
- For in-store purchases, the 14-day period is based on the purchase date on the receipt.
- For online purchases, the 14-day period is based on the tracking delivered date.

### What condition must returned items be in?

- Items must be new, unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition as sold or received.
- Online returns must be packaged securely.
- Return shipping must be prepaid by the customer and the parcel must be trackable.
- If possible, customers should reuse the same shipping box the order arrived in.

### What is issued for approved returns?

- Eligible returns are issued as store credit in the form of a gift card.
- For online returns, shipping fees are not refunded.
- A restocking fee may be applied if the item is not returned in the same condition it was received.

### Which items are final sale?

- Paints, thinners, pigments, markers, and consumables that do not have an additional seal
- Tools, paintbrushes, and items that cannot be verified as unused
- Sale items
- Clearance items
- Items marked final sale
- Collectibles
- Trading cards
- Blind boxes
- Exclusive items
- Limited edition items
- Panda Rewards redemption items
- All exchanged items are final sale

### Can shipping orders be canceled?

- Shipping orders that do not yet have a tracking number can be canceled.
- These cancellations are issued as store credit in the form of a gift card.

### Which orders cannot be canceled?

- Pre-orders
- Vault orders
- Pickup orders

### What happens with damaged or defective items?

- Items damaged in transit or arriving defective are eligible for an exchange for the same item or a replacement part, depending on the issue.
- Store credit may also be offered depending on the severity of the issue.
- Requests must be made within 14 days of receiving the order.
- Panda Hobby may ask for photos to determine whether a full replacement or replacement part is appropriate.

### What does not count as a defect?

- Minor cosmetic paint imperfections
- Packaging condition

### How are Panda Rewards redemption items valued in an exchange?

- Panda Rewards items are valued at the amount actually paid.
- Example: if an item was $100 and a $20 reward was applied, the exchange value is $80.

## Account and billing

### What if the customer already placed an order and wants to add more?

- If the first order has not shipped yet, the customer can email Panda Hobby to combine the orders.
- The customer should provide both order numbers.

### What if the customer made a mistake with the order?

- If the order has already shipped, it cannot be changed.
- If the order has not been fulfilled yet, the customer can email with the order number and request an adjustment.

### What if something is missing from the order?

- Ask the customer to check inside all boxes first, since smaller items like decals or markers may be packed inside kit boxes.
- If the item is still missing, direct the customer to email customerservice@pandahobby.ca for help.

### Do you sell replacement parts?

- No. Panda Hobby does not carry replacement parts and cannot order them directly.

### What if there is a defect in the product itself?

- Panda Hobby can contact the manufacturer on the customer’s behalf.
- The customer should send a photo to customerservice@pandahobby.ca.

### What should we say about pending charges when an order did not go through?

- Banks may place a pending charge or authorization hold during checkout even if the order does not complete.
- Panda Hobby only captures payment when the order is processed properly.
- If the order did not go through, the hold is released by the customer’s bank.
- The release timing usually takes 2 to 14 business days depending on the bank, though sometimes it clears much faster.
- Panda Hobby does not control this timing if no successful order was created.

## Inventory and availability

### When will a specific item be restocked?

- Japanese manufacturers often produce items in scheduled batches rather than continuously.
- Some kits may reprint every few months, while others may return much less often.
- Panda Hobby does not know manufacturer production schedules in advance.
- As an authorized reseller, Panda Hobby tries to bring in all available products it can secure.

### How can a customer be notified when an item is back in stock?

- Customers should use the Notify Me button on the product page and enter their email address.

### Are online and in-store inventories synced?

- Yes. Online and in-store inventory are synced.
- Product pages show which location has stock.
- If all locations show out of stock, the item is not available in store.

### Can Panda Hobby hold an item for a customer?

- The only guaranteed way to reserve an item is to buy it online and choose in-store pickup.

## Gift cards, promos, and rewards

### Can a customer use a gift card and another payment method together online?

- Yes. Customers can pay part of the order with a gift card and the rest with another payment method.

### What are Panda Rewards?

- Panda Rewards is Panda Hobby’s loyalty program.
- Customers earn 1 point for every dollar spent online or in store when using the same email address.
- Points can be redeemed for rewards, including discounts and special items.

### Can Panda Rewards be used on all products?

- No. Panda Rewards are not applicable to sale items, promotional items, or pre-order items.

### Can customers have more than one rewards account?

- No. One account per person.
- Duplicate accounts are not eligible for points redemption.

### Can points be transferred between accounts?

- No. Points cannot be transferred between accounts.

### What if a customer used different emails online and in store?

- Panda Hobby can update the in-store email to match the online account email.
- Account-linking requests are handled over email.

## Community and contact

### What should we say if a customer is waiting for a reply?

- Customer service replies are handled Monday to Friday from 12 PM to 7 PM EST.
- Email, Facebook, and Instagram replies are handled during working hours only.
- Panda Hobby does not reply after hours, on weekends, or on holidays.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate instead of drafting a final answer when:

- The issue involves a legal threat, chargeback, or fraud claim.
- The customer is requesting an exception outside written policy.
- The issue involves repeated missing parcels, customs seizure, or a high-value loss.
- The customer’s situation does not fit the current written FAQ or policy.
- The issue involves harassment, safety, or public reputation risk.
