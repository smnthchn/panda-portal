# Panda Hobby Customer Support SOPs

This document defines how Panda Hobby should handle common support situations and how the AI should draft replies for human review.

This is a draft-only workflow. A human always reviews and sends the final message.

## Support philosophy

Panda Hobby support should feel:

- Professional
- Warm
- Clear
- Helpful
- Human

Customers should feel like they are talking to a real person who understands the hobby, takes their concern seriously, and wants to help resolve it.

The goal is not just to answer questions. The goal is to make the hobby feel less frustrating and less lonely.

## General response SOP

Use this flow for every support email, DM, or written reply.

1. Read the full message before responding.
2. Identify the customer’s main issue.
3. Identify whether there is a second hidden issue such as confusion, frustration, or urgency.
4. Check the FAQ or written policy before stating facts.
5. Draft the response in Panda Hobby voice.
6. Answer the main question first.
7. Add the clearest next step.
8. If anything is uncertain, flag it for human review instead of guessing.

## Core writing rules

- Sound like a person, not a script.
- Be friendly, but stay organized.
- Lead with the answer when possible.
- Use empathy when something went wrong.
- Do not sound overly apologetic or robotic.
- Do not bury the action item.
- Do not use policy language in a cold or legal way.
- Do not invent information to sound helpful.

## Approved tone examples

- Good: Oh no, that’s not good. Let me help you with that.
- Good: Thanks for checking in. I took a look and here’s what’s going on.
- Good: Happy to help with that.
- Avoid: We apologize for the inconvenience.
- Avoid: Per our policy.
- Avoid: Your ticket has been received.

## SOP: shipping delay or stalled tracking

### Trigger

- Customer says the tracking is not updating.
- Customer says the order is taking too long.
- Customer asks where the package is.

### Goal

- Reassure the customer.
- Explain only the facts we can support.
- Give the customer a clear next step.

### Draft structure

1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. Explain that tracking may not update right away.
3. Confirm that a tracking number means the parcel has already shipped.
4. Set expectations using current policy or shipping estimates.
5. Offer the next action if the delay continues.

### Include when relevant

- Orders usually ship within 24 to 48 hours.
- Orders ship Monday to Friday only.
- Tracking may not update until the parcel reaches the local shipping hub.
- Some regions take longer than others.

### Do not

- Promise an exact delivery date unless verified.
- Blame the courier.
- Tell the customer to “just wait” without context.

## SOP: order not shipped yet

### Trigger

- Customer says the order still shows unfulfilled.
- Customer asks why they have not received tracking yet.

### Goal

- Determine whether this is normal processing, a pre-order issue, a Vault issue, or a problem that needs review.

### Draft structure

1. Confirm the order is being checked.
2. Clarify the most likely reason for the delay.
3. Tell the customer what to check or what Panda Hobby will check next.

### Check for

- Whether the order contains a pre-order item
- Whether the customer selected Panda Vault
- Whether order information is missing or needs correction
- Whether the order was placed on a weekend or holiday

## SOP: pre-order questions

### Trigger

- Customer asks what pre-order means.
- Customer asks when a pre-order will arrive.
- Customer asks why the full order has not shipped yet.
- Customer asks to split shipment or pickup.

### Goal

- Set expectations clearly.
- Avoid promising timing that Panda Hobby cannot guarantee.

### Draft structure

1. Explain the relevant pre-order rule clearly.
2. Reference the estimated release timing as an estimate only.
3. Explain what happens once the item arrives.
4. Offer the best next step if the customer wants items sooner.

### Key facts

- Orders containing a pre-order ship or become available for pickup only when all items in that order are available.
- Partial shipment and partial pickup are not available for mixed pre-order orders.
- Release timing is based on manufacturer estimates and may change.
- Customers pay for standard pre-orders when the order is placed.

### Do not

- Promise exact arrival timing.
- Suggest that Panda Hobby controls manufacturer delays.
- Offer partial shipment unless policy changes and is confirmed.

## SOP: refund, return, or exchange request

### Trigger

- Customer wants to return, exchange, or cancel an item.

### Goal

- Apply the current policy accurately while still sounding respectful and helpful.

### Draft structure

1. Acknowledge the request.
2. State whether the item is eligible.
3. Give the relevant condition or time window.
4. Explain the next step clearly.

### Key facts

- Eligible returns and exchanges are accepted within 14 days.
- Eligible returns are issued as store credit in the form of a gift card.
- Many categories are final sale, including sale items, clearance items, collectibles, trading cards, blind boxes, exclusive items, limited edition items, many consumables, and Panda Rewards redemption items.
- Exchange items become final sale.

### Do not

- Quote policy in a harsh or legal tone.
- Promise cash refunds unless confirmed by policy.
- Create exceptions without human review.

## SOP: cancellation request

### Trigger

- Customer asks to cancel an order.

### Goal

- Determine whether the order is eligible and communicate the outcome clearly.

### Key facts

- Shipping orders without a tracking number can be canceled for store credit.
- Pre-orders cannot be canceled once placed if policy says so.
- Vault orders cannot be canceled.
- Pickup orders cannot be canceled under the current draft policy.

### When to escalate

- The cancellation policy for the specific order type is unclear.
- The customer disputes a policy application.
- The order is unusually high value or commercially sensitive.

## SOP: order edits and address changes

### Trigger

- Customer wants to add an item.
- Customer wants to update the shipping address.
- Customer made a mistake on the order.

### Goal

- Move quickly before fulfillment happens.

### Draft structure

1. Confirm whether the order has already shipped.
2. If not shipped, tell the customer to send the order number and requested change.
3. If shipped, explain that the order can no longer be changed.

### Key facts

- If the order has already shipped, it cannot be changed.
- If the order has not been fulfilled, the customer can email with the order number to request an adjustment.
- If a customer wants to add more items and the first order has not shipped, Panda Hobby may be able to combine orders.

## SOP: missing item

### Trigger

- Customer says an item is missing from the package.

### Goal

- Resolve the issue calmly and efficiently.

### Draft structure

1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. Ask the customer to check all kit boxes and packaging first.
3. Mention that smaller items like decals or markers may be packed inside kit boxes.
4. If still missing, direct the customer to email support for resolution.

## SOP: damaged or defective item

### Trigger

- Customer reports transit damage.
- Customer says an item arrived defective.

### Goal

- Show empathy and gather what is needed to review the issue properly.

### Draft structure

1. Acknowledge the problem.
2. Ask for photos if they have not already been provided.
3. Explain that Panda Hobby will review whether a replacement, replacement part, or store credit is appropriate.

### Key facts

- Requests must be made within 14 days of receiving the order.
- Minor cosmetic paint imperfections and packaging condition do not count as product defects.
- Panda Hobby may contact the manufacturer on the customer’s behalf for product defects.

## SOP: pending charge or payment issue

### Trigger

- Customer says the order failed but they see a charge.
- Customer says a debit or credit payment is still pending.

### Goal

- Explain the bank hold process clearly and reduce customer anxiety.

### Draft structure

1. Explain that the charge is usually a pending authorization from the bank.
2. Clarify that Panda Hobby only captures payment when an order is processed successfully.
3. Explain that release timing is controlled by the customer’s bank.
4. Suggest checking with the bank if the hold lasts too long.

### Key facts

- Release timing is typically 2 to 14 business days depending on the bank.
- Panda Hobby does not control the release if no successful order was created.

## SOP: stock and restock questions

### Trigger

- Customer asks when a specific item will be back.
- Customer asks whether out-of-stock online items are available in store.

### Goal

- Be informative without pretending to know manufacturer schedules we do not have.

### Draft structure

1. Explain that reprints and restocks follow manufacturer production schedules.
2. Clarify that Panda Hobby does not receive full production calendars in advance.
3. Point the customer to the Notify Me option when appropriate.
4. Confirm that online and in-store inventory are synced.

## SOP: customer wants an item held

### Trigger

- Customer asks to reserve an item.

### Goal

- Offer the approved path without making informal promises.

### Key facts

- The only guaranteed way to reserve an item is to buy it online and choose in-store pickup.

## SOP: Panda Vault questions

### Trigger

- Customer asks what Vault is.
- Customer asks why the order has not shipped.
- Customer asks how to ship stored orders.

### Goal

- Explain the Vault clearly and point to the next action.

### Key facts

- Panda Vault lets customers hold and combine orders.
- Orders can be stored for 60 days.
- Customers request shipment when ready.
- Vault orders cannot be canceled under the current draft policy.

## SOP: pickup questions

### Trigger

- Customer asks how pickup works.
- Customer asks when pickup is ready.
- Customer selected pickup by mistake.

### Goal

- Keep the process clear and simple.

### Key facts

- Customers must wait for the READY FOR PICKUP email before coming in.
- Pickup may take up to 2 hours during operating hours.
- If pickup was chosen by mistake, Panda Hobby can invoice for shipping or help combine with another order.

## SOP: rewards and account issues

### Trigger

- Customer asks about Panda Rewards.
- Customer used the wrong email.
- Customer asks about combining accounts or transferring points.

### Goal

- Keep answers simple and factual.

### Key facts

- Customers earn 1 point per dollar spent when using the same email online and in store.
- Rewards cannot be transferred between accounts.
- One account per person.
- Rewards are not valid on sale items, promotional items, or pre-orders.

## SOP: upset customer or complaint

### Trigger

- Customer is angry, disappointed, accusatory, or losing trust.

### Goal

- De-escalate while staying clear and grounded.

### Draft structure

1. Acknowledge the frustration.
2. Confirm the issue is being looked into.
3. State the next step clearly.
4. Keep the tone calm and confident.

### Do not

- Match the customer’s hostility
- Sound sarcastic or dismissive
- Overexplain before acknowledging the problem
- Make promises just to calm the situation

## Escalation SOP

Escalate to Samantha or Baldwin when:

- The customer threatens legal action, chargeback, or public escalation.
- The customer asks for an exception outside written policy.
- The facts are unclear and the AI or drafter would need to guess.
- The issue involves customs seizure, a high-value order, or repeated shipping failure.
- The issue involves harassment, discrimination, or safety concerns.
- The reply could materially affect reputation or financial outcome.

## AI drafting SOP

When the AI drafts a reply, it must:

1. Use the Brand Voice Guide for tone.
2. Use the Customer Support FAQ for facts.
3. Avoid inventing any policy, date, shipment promise, or product detail.
4. Flag uncertain cases with `Needs human review`.
5. Produce a clean draft a human can send with light editing.

## Final QA checklist

Before a human sends the draft, confirm:

- The facts match the current written policy.
- The tone sounds like Panda Hobby.
- The customer’s actual question was answered.
- The next step is clear.
- Nothing has been promised that Panda Hobby may not be able to do.
- The response sounds human, warm, and competent.
