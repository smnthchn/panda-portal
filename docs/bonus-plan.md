# In-store sales bonus plan

Staff earn a bonus when in-store sales pass a goal, and the portal shows them
how close they are. Planned and built 2026-11-20. Open questions are in
HANDOFF.md, not here.

Staff are written by their portal staff ID here, never by name, and the
goals are linked to people the same way (Sam, 2026-11-20). Staff 5 works
Monday to Friday. Staff 4 and Staff 6 work the weekends. Who is who is on the
portal's Staff page.

## Non-negotiable

- **Refunds, cancellations and anything else that takes money back off a
  sale must come off the sales total.** (Sam, 2026-11-20)

## Decided

- **It runs for November and December 2026 only.** It's an incentive for
  those 2 months. It runs all year only if it turns out very successful.
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **Tracking starts Saturday, Nov 21, 2026.** The first weekend is Nov 21 to
  22 and Staff 5's first week is Nov 23 to 27. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **Each staff member sees their weekly goal on the portal.**
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **The portal holds the numbers and works out the goals and bonuses, and
  shows sales so far from Shopify.** The sales figure goes up by itself as
  sales come in, at most two minutes behind. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **Boss-level users get a section to see and change each goal amount.** It
  shows the matching 2025 sales beside each week and weekend. Staff do not
  see the 2025 sales. (Sam, 2026-11-20) Boss-level means the portal's `boss`
  role. (Claude, 2026-11-20)
- **What staff see is a cute, fun meter that fills up as sales come in,**
  with a mark at every $500 so they can see how close the next step is. It
  does not reuse the break battery's look. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **The meter is a Christmas tree,** since this is a holiday bonus. The tree
  fills with green as sales grow toward the goal. When the goal is hit the
  star lights up. Past the goal, the string lights on the tree come on as
  sales go. It uses the tree picture Sam sent, not a drawing of Claude's.
  (Sam, 2026-11-20) Sam saw it built on the staff card and signed it off:
  "love it!" (Sam, 2026-11-20) The picture is hers to use: she made it in
  ChatGPT on her paid subscription. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **A light is white, with no colour, until sales pass its amount. Once
  sales pass it, it is coloured.** (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **Past the goal, one light is $250 of sales,** so two lights make the next
  $25. (Sam, 2026-11-24) The picture has 22 lights, which shows up to $5,500
  past a goal. It was $100 a light at first, and the first weekend went
  $2,417 past its goal and ran out of lights.
- **The meter carries numbers,** so staff can see what point they have got
  up to and how much more they need to sell to reach the goal.
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **The card explains the bonus in the tree's own words:** "Fill the tree
  all the way to the star and receive a $XX bonus! Then for every 2
  Christmas lights you light up, you'll get an additional $XX bonus."
  (Sam, 2026-11-21; it said "every 5" until a light became $250 on
  2026-11-24)
- **Staff are only ever told their own amount, never a total that gets
  split.** A total makes people think it is all theirs, which is confusion
  nobody needs. So the weekend card says "$60 bonus", not $120, and each
  further step is $12.50, not $25. (Sam, 2026-11-21) Every dollar figure on
  a person's card is their own, worked out from the days they are down as
  working, and the totals are not sent to a staff member's browser at all.
  The boss's copy of the card shows the per-person figure, and the full
  breakdown is on the Sales bonus page. (Claude, 2026-11-21)
- **The tree has a little space above the star and below the trunk.**
  (Sam, 2026-11-21)
- **After a goal ends, staff see only the bonus they made,** not the sales
  that were done. (Sam, 2026-11-21) The finished goal's sales figure is not
  sent to a staff member's browser. While a goal is running they still see
  sales so far, since that is what fills the tree. (Claude, 2026-11-21)
- **Staff see one goal at a time:** the one running now, or the very next
  one when none is running. They never see the full list, so later goals can
  be changed on the Sales bonus page before anyone has seen them. The next
  goal starts showing the moment the current one ends. (Claude, 2026-11-21)
- **It's a group goal, not a per-person one.** Every POS sale at the Markham
  store counts, whoever rang it up. The portal doesn't show who sold what.
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **In-store sales at Markham only.** That means sales rung up on the POS at
  the Markham location. Online orders, draft orders, and the Warehouse and
  Events & Conventions POS locations don't count, for the bonus or for the
  past-year averages. (Sam, 2026-11-20) Days when a convention was rung up
  under the Markham location are left out for the same reason.
  (Claude, 2026-11-20)
- **Two goals a week.** Monday to Friday is Staff 5's, the only one
  who works those days. Saturday and Sunday is shared between Staff 4 and 6.
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **When a goal is met, every person gets $20 for each day they worked in
  it.** The goals stay weekly and weekend goals; there are no daily goals.
  The $20 is the same on any day, weekday or weekend, for anyone. It is one
  amount per person and is not split: two people working the same day both
  get $20. (Sam, 2026-11-21) So a full five-day week is $100, a four-day
  week $80, and a full weekend $40 each.
- **Every further $500 past the goal adds $25, and that extra is shared
  between the people who worked, by days worked.** (Sam, 2026-11-21) Sam's
  example: weekend staff helps on one weekday sale day and the week ends $50
  of extra over; six days were worked in all, so the weekday staff gets
  $100 plus $41.67 and the helper $20 plus $8.33.
  The first weekend, Nov 21 to 22, is $20 a day like every other goal: it
  was live under the old rule for a few hours, but Sam had not told any
  staff and none had looked. (Sam, 2026-11-21)
- **A person is paid only for the days they actually worked.**
  (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **The boss says who worked.** The usual staff for a goal are ticked as
  working each day by default. When someone was off, the boss unticks that
  day. Anyone else can be added to a single day, such as weekend staff
  helping on a weekday. This stands in until the clock and the schedule are
  finished. (Sam, 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21)
- **Sales are counted before tax and after discounts.** A $100 kit sold with
  $10 off counts as $90. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **A refund or cancellation comes off the week or weekend the sale was made
  in.** (Claude, 2026-11-20) That it comes off at all is a non-negotiable,
  above.
- **Every goal is last year's sales for the same days, minus 5%,** weekdays
  and weekends alike, the first weekend included. (Sam, 2026-11-21: "Change
  them to 5% under") This is settled: do not offer other goal levels again
  unless Sam reopens it.
  - **Weekday goals:** $5,700 (Nov 23 to 27), $5,110 (Nov 30 to Dec 4),
    $6,220 (Dec 7 to 11), $7,150 (Dec 14 to 18), $12,260 (Dec 21 to 24) and
    $9,540 (Dec 28 to 31).
    UNDECIDED: whether the goals for Dec 1 to 19 need lowering: they are
    built on last year's sale days, see HANDOFF.md
  - **Weekend goals:** $5,900 (Nov 21 to 22), $6,170 (Nov 28 to 29), $4,590
    (Dec 5 to 6), $7,110 (Dec 12 to 13), $8,210 (Dec 19 to 20) and $9,770
    (Dec 26 to 27).
  Why 5% and not 10%: with sales 10% over last year, the whole period pays
  about $1,565 in bonuses at 5% under, against $1,815 at 10% under. The $20
  a day is the same either way; a higher goal leaves less room for the extra
  tiers.
- **Goals are built day by day, then added up for the week.** Each day is
  matched to its own day last year, so a big day that moves between the
  weekday goal and the weekend goal lands in the right one. Boxing Day was a
  Friday in 2025 and is a Saturday in 2026. (Sam, 2026-11-20)
  How a day is matched: the same weekday last year (364 days back). From
  Dec 21 to Dec 31 it is the same date instead, so Christmas Eve, Christmas
  Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Eve each match themselves.
  (Claude, 2026-11-20)
- **Goals are rounded to the nearest $10.** (Sam, 2026-11-20)
- **The last weekday goal covers Dec 28 to 31, and the last weekend is Dec 26
  and 27.** Jan 2 and 3 fall outside the two months. (Claude, 2026-11-20)

## Build

Sam's go on 2026-11-20: build it now, with a placeholder where the meter's
final look will go. Everything below is Claude's choice inside that
(Claude, 2026-11-20).

- **The numbers live in the portal's database,** one row per week or
  weekend (`bonus_blocks`): dates, goal, what each person gets per day
  worked when the goal is met (`day_rate_cents`), the step ($500) and what a
  step adds ($25), and the matching 2025 sales. A goal left empty shows
  staff "Goal not set yet". The older `base_cents` column is no longer read.
- **Who usually works each kind of goal is a list** (`bonus_people`):
  Staff 5 on weekdays, Staff 4 and 6 on weekends. The boss can change it.
- **Who worked is a tick box per person per day** on the boss page. The
  usual staff start ticked and only their unticks are stored
  (`bonus_absences`). Anyone else is added to a single day with "+ Add" and
  stored in `bonus_extra_days`; unticking them takes them off again. A tick
  saves the moment it is changed. A helper sees that goal's card only while
  they are down for one of its days. Staff see their own amount and nothing
  about anyone else's days.
- **Boss page: Sales bonus,** in the sidebar and under More. Each week and
  weekend shows its 2025 sales, goal, pay, sales so far and bonus earned. The
  goal and pay can be changed there. Only the `boss` role can open it, and the
  2025 sales are never sent to anyone else's browser.
- **Staff card on Home,** for the people on a list: this week's or weekend's
  goal, sales so far, how far to the goal or the next $25, and the bonus so
  far. Before the first day it shows the coming goal with the tree still
  white.
- **The boss sees the staff cards too,** on their own Home screen, one for
  weekdays and one for the weekend, exactly as staff see them, with a button
  through to the Sales bonus page. Sam asked for a staff view in boss mode
  after the first deploy (Sam, 2026-11-20). The boss is on no list, so the
  card shows them no share.
- **The tree is one picture drawn three ways at once** (`bonusMeterHtml()`
  in `public/bonus.js`, picture `public/bonus-tree.webp`): washed out all
  over; in full colour from the foot up to how far sales have got; and each
  bulb washed back to white until sales pass it. The bulbs' and star's
  places were measured from the picture and are listed in `BONUS_TREE`; a
  different picture needs them measured again. The picture has a white
  background, so the tree sits on a white panel in every theme.
- **Sales come from Shopify each time a page asks,** and the answer is kept
  for 100 seconds, so the card is at most two minutes behind. The card asks
  again every two minutes while it is open. A finished week is re-checked at
  most once an hour, which is how a late refund still comes off.
- **What counts:** in-store orders at the Markham location, by the day the
  sale was made (Toronto time). An order's amount is its subtotal after
  discounts, returns and refunds. A cancelled order counts as nothing. Money
  refunded with no item returned comes off too, less its share of tax.

## What we know

Measured from Shopify on 2026-11-20. Sales here are **net sales**: before tax,
after discounts and returns. With-tax figures are about 13% higher.

- **Shopify already records what's needed.** POS sales carry a location and a
  staff name. The store has three POS locations: `Markham (Shipping & Pickup)`,
  `Warehouse` and `Events & Conventions`. Only the first one is the store.
- **Staff 6 goes by a different first name on the POS than on the portal.**
  Staff 4, Staff 5 and Baldwin have the same name in both. This only matters
  if sales are ever split by who rang them up.
- **Baldwin rings up a quarter of weekday sales.** Markham, Monday to Friday,
  Sep 21 to Nov 15, 2026: Staff 5 71%, Baldwin 25%, Staff 6 3%, the shared "Panda
  Staff" login 1%. Under the group rule those all count toward the weekday goal.
- **A normal weekday week is under $5,000 right now.** The 8 weeks Sep 21 to
  Nov 15, 2026: Monday to Friday averaged $4,072 (lowest $3,176, highest
  $5,275). The example rule would have paid $0 in all 8 weeks.
- **December is well over $5,000.** Monday to Friday, Nov 17, 2025 to Jan 2,
  2026, ran $5,380 to $13,277 a week. A flat $5,000 goal would have paid $0 to
  $400 a week ($400 was Christmas week), $875 over the 7 weeks. The same weeks
  in 2024 ran $6,599 to $20,473.
- **Why the goal is not an average of the last 2 years.** Sales have fallen
  two years running, so that average sits above what the store does now: for
  the week of Nov 23 it is $8,052 against $5,998 last year. Tested on November
  and December 2025 it would have paid Staff 5 $25 in 9 weeks.
- **Discounts are big, returns are small.** Markham POS, November 2025: full
  price $59,144, discounts $7,868 (13%), returns $211, leaving $51,065.
  December 2025: $75,575, discounts $5,141 (7%), returns $301, leaving $70,133.

- **The portal's Shopify app can read Markham in-store sales** (tested
  2026-11-20 with the app's own token, read only). Sam added `read_orders`
  and `read_all_orders` to the Panda Portal app on 2026-11-20. No Worker code
  calls Shopify yet. How: the Admin GraphQL `orders` query with the search
  `source_name:pos location_id:3164241943` (Markham) and a `created_at`
  range, adding up `currentSubtotalPriceSet`. That matched Shopify's net
  sales to the cent on 6 days (Nov 7, 14, 15, 19 and 20, 2026, and a
  convention day, May 23, 2026, where Markham read $0 and Events &
  Conventions $25,551.56). The built portal code was then run on this
  computer against the live store: the weekend of Nov 14 to 15 read $2,873.91
  and the week of Nov 16 to 20 read $3,691.64, both equal to Shopify's
  report. The week of Nov 2 to 6 read $3,126.74 against Shopify's $3,176.47:
  $49.99 of that is the cancelled order RX173610, left out on purpose, and
  $0.26 is a return on Nov 5 that Shopify values a little differently.
  Gotchas: the dates in the search must be in quotes
  (`created_at:>='2026-11-14T00:00:00-05:00'`), or the end date is read as a
  whole day and two days come back. The `retailLocation` field is refused (it
  needs `read_locations`); the `location_id:` search works without it. A
  return comes off the day of the original sale here, where Shopify's report
  takes it off the day of the return, so a week can differ by a returned
  item. Toronto is UTC-5 in winter and UTC-4 in summer.
  `shopify-app/shopify.app.toml` lists the two scopes (added to the file
  2026-11-20, not committed yet), so a release from the repo keeps them.
- **What `currentSubtotalPriceSet` takes off by itself, and what it misses**
  (checked on real in-store orders, 2026-11-20). Taken off: an item returned
  or refunded (RX171280, $49.99 down to $0; RX160132, $49.59 down to $15.60),
  and a voided order. Missed, so the build needs its own rule for each:
  an order cancelled without a refund still shows its full amount (RX173610,
  Nov 2, 2026, $49.99, cancelled Nov 6; about one in-store order a year), so
  leave out any order with a `cancelledAt`; and money refunded with no item
  returned, such as a price adjustment, does not lower it (RX158841, $17.96
  refunded, still $107.08), so take those refunds off from the order's
  `refunds`. Check the result against Shopify's own report during the build.
- **The portal's clock can't say who worked yet.** Nobody has clocked in on
  the live portal in the 30 days to 2026-11-20 (checked on production, read
  only): not Staff 4, 5 or 6, and not Baldwin. Shopify does record who rang up
  each sale, but reading it needs another permission (`read_users`), and the
  shared "Panda Staff" login and Baldwin's sales muddy it.
- **Ties to the payroll plan** (`docs/payroll-plan.md`): Phase 2 has manual
  bonus entries on a person's pay timeline, and the Phase 3 estimate leaves
  bonuses out.
